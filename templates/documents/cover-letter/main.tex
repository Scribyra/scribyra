Dear Ms Hiring Manager,

Open with the role you are applying for and one sentence on why you are a good
fit. Mention where you saw the advertisement, or who referred you.

In the second paragraph, give two or three concrete examples from your
experience that match what the employer asks for. Numbers and results are more
convincing than adjectives.

In the third paragraph, explain why you want to work for this organisation in
particular, and what you would bring to the team.

Close by thanking the reader and saying that you look forward to discussing
the role. Mention any enclosures.
